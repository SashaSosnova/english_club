# Level 2 — Everyday English (A2)

**Уроков:** 28 (7 units × 4)  
**Длина урока:** 30–40 мин  
**Цель уровня:** объясняться в путешествиях; рассказывать истории; понимать простые истории и короткие клипы с EN-субтитрами.

**К концу уровня ученик умеет:**
- check-in, заказ в ресторане, транспорт, покупки, базовая медпомощь;
- рассказать отпуск / проблему в Past + Present Perfect (базово);
- выразить мнение и совет;
- читать graded reader A2 (короткие главы);
- понимать 30–90 сек video clips с EN-subs (gist).

---

## Unit 1 — Arrangements & past stories

### Lesson 1 — Present Continuous for future
- **Grammar:** Present Continuous for fixed arrangements
- **Vocab (12):** meet, fly, leave, arrive, stay, book, appointment, calendar, tonight, this weekend, invite, join
- **Practice:** diary gaps → make arrangements dialogue
- **Production:** «My plans this week» (arrangements)
- **Input:** audio — two friends planning Friday
- **Travel:** *I'm flying on Monday. I'm staying at…*

### Lesson 2 — Past Simple expansion
- **Grammar:** Past Simple review + time markers (yesterday, last, ago, in 2019)
- **Vocab (12):** trip, journey, flight, delayed, missed, arrived, spent, enjoyed, crowded, quiet, expensive, cheap
- **Practice:** timeline cloze → irregular refresh → retell
- **Production:** short trip story (8 предложений)
- **Input:** listening airport delay story

### Lesson 3 — used to (light)
- **Grammar:** used to + V (past habits / states); didn't use to; questions
- **Vocab (12):** used to, habit, change, before, child, school, smoke, eat meat, live abroad, afraid, shy, different
- **Practice:** then vs now pairs → error fix
- **Production:** 5 *I used to…* about your life
- **Input:** text «I used to live in…»
- **Literature seed:** character past habits in graded text

### Lesson 4 — will vs going to + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** will (spontaneous / predictions) vs going to (plans / evidence)
- **Vocab (12):** probably, maybe, I think, decision, promise, weather, forecast, sure, not sure, later, soon, someday
- **Practice:** choose will/going to → predictions about photos
- **Production:** plans + 2 predictions
- **Unit Challenge:** weekend arrangements + past trip + future plans mix
- **Input:** weather forecast audio (simplified)

## Unit 2 — Comparing, advice & possibility

### Lesson 5 — Comparatives
- **Grammar:** comparative adj (-er / more); irregular better, worse, further
- **Vocab (12):** bigger, smaller, cheaper, more expensive, better, worse, faster, slower, more interesting, more comfortable, than, much
- **Practice:** compare hotels/cities → sentence builder
- **Production:** compare 2 cities or 2 hotels
- **Travel:** choosing a hotel / ticket

### Lesson 6 — Superlatives
- **Grammar:** the -est / the most; irregular best, worst
- **Vocab (12):** the best, the worst, the biggest, the most popular, the nearest, the furthest, ever, in the world, in my life, restaurant, beach, view
- **Practice:** ranking tasks → opinion gaps
- **Production:** «The best trip / meal in my life»
- **Input:** review-style text about a city

### Lesson 7 — should / must / have to
- **Grammar:** should (advice); must / have to (obligation); mustn't vs don't have to
- **Vocab (12):** should, must, have to, advice, rule, ticket, passport, visa, queue, on time, careful, important
- **Practice:** airport rules → advice for tourists
- **Production:** 5 советов другу перед поездкой
- **Travel focus:** *You have to show your passport. You shouldn't…*

### Lesson 8 — may / might + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** may / might for possibility
- **Vocab (12):** possible, possible not, chance, depend, traffic, rain, cancel, open, closed, available, full, free
- **Practice:** might-dialogues → soften answers
- **Production:** планы с неопределённостью (*I might…*)
- **Unit Challenge:** compare options + give advice + maybe-plans
- **Input:** hotel receptionist dialogue (might be full)

## Unit 3 — Conditionals, Perfect & life experience

### Lesson 9 — First Conditional
- **Grammar:** If + Present Simple, will…
- **Vocab (12):** if, unless (intro), miss, catch, late, early, cancel, refund, hungry, tired, rain, shine
- **Practice:** complete chains → travel problems (*If I miss the train…*)
- **Production:** 6 travel conditionals
- **Input:** audio problem-solving dialogue

### Lesson 10 — Present Perfect intro
- **Grammar:** have/has + V3; ever / never; been to vs gone to (light)
- **Vocab (14):** ever, never, just, already, yet, experience, tried, visited, seen, eaten, flown, driven, before, so far
- **Practice:** ever/never questions → participle drills (core list)
- **Production:** «Have you ever…?» interview (6 Q)
- **Phrases:** *Have you ever been to…? I've never tried…*

### Lesson 11 — Present Perfect vs Past Simple
- **Grammar:** experience (PP) vs finished time (Past Simple)
- **Vocab (12):** last year, yesterday, ago, this week, today, recently, in 2020, when, time markers, still, already, yet
- **Practice:** choose tense → error clinic (RU typical)
- **Production:** experiences + when details
- **Input:** dialogue «Have you seen this film? — Yes, I saw it last month.»

### Lesson 12 — Travel story + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** mix Past Simple + Present Perfect + First Conditional
- **Vocab (12):** luggage, suitcase, lost, found, border, customs, stamp, gate, boarding pass, seat, aisle, window
- **Practice:** airport scenario tree → cloze story
- **Production:** «My travel experience» (10 предложений)
- **Unit Challenge:** quiz + speaking board: ever/never + advice + if-plans
- **Input:** first graded reader chapter (A2) — gist questions

## Unit 4 — Hotel, food & shopping scenarios

### Lesson 13 — something / anything / nothing / everything
- **Grammar:** some-/any-/no-/every- compounds (thing/body/where)
- **Vocab (12):** something, anything, nothing, everything, somebody, anybody, nobody, somewhere, anywhere, nowhere, else, wrong
- **Practice:** choose form → hotel complaint dialogue
- **Production:** report a small problem (*There's something wrong with…*)
- **Travel:** hotel issues

### Lesson 14 — too / enough
- **Grammar:** too + adj; (not) adj enough; too much/many; not enough
- **Vocab (12):** too hot, too cold, too small, too expensive, big enough, warm enough, spicy, quiet, loud, soft, hard, comfortable
- **Practice:** restaurant/hotel complaints → transform
- **Production:** feedback about a room/meal
- **Input:** review dialogue

### Lesson 15 — Restaurant & special requests
- **Grammar:** functional requests; I'd like / Could I… / without / instead of
- **Vocab (14):** starter, main course, dessert, allergy, vegetarian, vegan, gluten, spicy, bill, tip, separately, together, recommend, delicious
- **Practice:** order builder → listen for special requests
- **Production:** full restaurant dialogue (2–3 turns each)
- **Travel focus:** ordering with constraints

### Lesson 16 — Shopping & returns + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** review compounds + too/enough + polite requests
- **Vocab (14):** size, fit, try on, receipt, return, exchange, discount, sale, cash, card, cash machine, queue, broken, damaged
- **Practice:** shop dialogues → return scenario
- **Production:** «I'd like to return this…»
- **Unit Challenge:** hotel complaint + restaurant order + shop return
- **Input:** 45–60 сек shop clip (EN subs) + gist

## Unit 5 — People, places & purpose

### Lesson 17 — Relative pronouns (who / which / that)
- **Grammar:** defining relative clauses (basic)
- **Vocab (12):** who, which, that, person, place, thing, film, book, friend, city, job, gift
- **Practice:** join sentences → describe people/things
- **Production:** 6 sentences *… who/which/that …*
- **Literature seed:** describe a character simply

### Lesson 18 — Passive Present (light)
- **Grammar:** is/are + V3 (is made, is called, is spoken)
- **Vocab (12):** made, called, spoken, grown, used, known, served, produced, located, famous for, from, of
- **Practice:** active→passive (guided) → city facts
- **Production:** 5 facts about a country/city in passive
- **Input:** «English is spoken…» culture bites

### Lesson 19 — Gerunds after like / enjoy / hate
- **Grammar:** like/love/enjoy/hate/don't mind + V-ing
- **Vocab (12):** swimming, reading, travelling, cooking, waiting, getting up, shopping, hiking, watching series, learning, meeting people, staying in
- **Practice:** surveys → true sentences
- **Production:** hobbies talk (1–1.5 мин)
- **Film seed:** *I enjoy watching… / I don't mind reading subtitles.*

### Lesson 20 — Infinitive of purpose + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** to + V (purpose); go to / come to / need to
- **Vocab (12):** to buy, to see, to learn, to relax, to visit, to practise, to change, to ask, to find, reason, purpose, so that (preview)
- **Practice:** why-questions → purpose chains
- **Production:** «I'm learning English to…» + travel purposes
- **Unit Challenge:** describe people/places with relative clauses + likes + purposes
- **Input:** graded reader ch.2 + character who/which questions

## Unit 6 — Opinions, linking & health

### Lesson 21 — Articles review & traps
- **Grammar:** a/an/the/zero; common RU mistakes (the school vs school; go to hospital BrE note)
- **Vocab (12):** school, work, home, bed, hospital, cinema, station, same, only, next, last, both
- **Practice:** article clinic → error fix in diary
- **Production:** corrected short paragraph about yesterday
- **Input:** edited learner text

### Lesson 22 — Linking words
- **Grammar / discourse:** and, but, because, so, then, after that, however (light), for example
- **Vocab (12):** because, so, then, after that, first, finally, however, for example, also, too, as well, suddenly
- **Practice:** join story parts → improve choppy text
- **Production:** connected weekend story (10–12 предложений)
- **Literature seed:** sequencing in stories

### Lesson 23 — Doctor & pharmacy
- **Grammar:** should + symptoms language; Present Perfect for recent symptoms (*I've had a headache since…* light)
- **Vocab (14):** headache, sore throat, cough, fever, cold, pain, stomach, allergy, medicine, pharmacy, prescription, appointment, rest, better
- **Practice:** symptoms→advice → pharmacy dialogue
- **Production:** explain how you feel + ask for help
- **Travel focus:** clinic / pharmacy survival

### Lesson 24 — Emotions & opinions + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** opinion phrases; agree / disagree politely
- **Vocab (14):** happy, upset, nervous, excited, bored, angry, worried, surprised, I think, in my opinion, agree, disagree, maybe, absolutely
- **Practice:** react to situations → film/book opinion mini
- **Production:** opinion on a short clip/story (8 предложений)
- **Unit Challenge:** tell a linked story + health dialogue + opinion
- **Input:** 60–90 сек clip with EN subs — mood/gist questions
