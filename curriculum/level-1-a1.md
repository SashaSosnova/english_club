# Level 1 — Foundations (A1)

**Уроков:** 24 (6 units × 4)  
**Длина урока:** 30–40 мин  
**Цель уровня:** говорить о себе, дне, еде, городе; понимать медленную речь; читать короткие тексты; базовые travel-фразы.

**К концу уровня ученик умеет:**
- представиться, рассказать о семье, работе, рутине;
- заказать еду, спросить цену и дорогу;
- описать планы и короткий прошлый уикенд;
- понимать slow dialogue и micro-text 80–120 слов.

---

## Unit 1 — Me & people around me

### Lesson 1 — Hello & to be
- **Grammar:** am / is / are; negatives (I'm not…); Yes/No questions (Are you…?)
- **Vocab (12):** hello, hi, goodbye, name, nice to meet you, from, country, city, yes, no, please, thank you
- **Practice:** form drills → reorder → error fix (*I is…*) → mini-dialogue «At a language club»
- **Production:** 4–5 предложений о себе (голос или текст)
- **Input:** audio 40 сек — знакомство
- **Phrases:** *Nice to meet you. Where are you from?*

### Lesson 2 — a/an & things around you
- **Grammar:** a/an; this / that
- **Vocab (12):** pen, book, phone, bag, table, chair, door, window, water, coffee, ticket, map
- **Practice:** a/an choose → point & say (this/that) → cloze
- **Production:** назвать 6 предметов вокруг себя
- **Travel seed:** *This is my ticket / passport.*
- **Input:** short audio — «What's this?»

### Lesson 3 — Family & possessives
- **Grammar:** my / your / his / her / our / their; possessive 's
- **Vocab (12):** mother, father, sister, brother, husband, wife, child, children, friend, parents, family, people
- **Practice:** match photo→word → 's drills → sentence builder
- **Production:** описать 2 человек из семьи
- **Input:** text 50 слов — «My family»

### Lesson 4 — Numbers, time & Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** What's the time?; at + time; on + day (light)
- **Vocab (14):** one–twenty, thirty, forty, fifty, hundred; o'clock, half past, morning, afternoon, evening, today, tomorrow, weekend
- **Practice:** listen & choose time → say the time → dialogue «When do we meet?»
- **Unit Challenge:** quiz Units 1 + speaking: представить себя и назвать время встречи
- **Input:** audio — время и договорённость

## Unit 2 — Home, food & having things

### Lesson 5 — Plurals & these/those
- **Grammar:** regular plurals (-s/-es); these / those; irregular: men, women, children, people
- **Vocab (12):** apple, orange, sandwich, key, bus, buses, man, woman, child, people, shoes, glasses
- **Practice:** singular→plural → these/those → error fix
- **Production:** описать картинку стола с едой/вещами
- **Input:** audio listing items

### Lesson 6 — There is / there are
- **Grammar:** There is / There are; Isn't / Aren't; Is there…? Are there…?
- **Vocab (12):** room, kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, fridge, sofa, TV, lamp, plant, picture, floor, wall
- **Practice:** gap fill по плану квартиры → Yes/No questions
- **Production:** «In my room there is / there are…» (6 предложений)
- **Input:** text 60 слов — описание комнаты

### Lesson 7 — have got
- **Grammar:** have got / has got; haven't / hasn't; Have you got…?
- **Vocab (12):** car, bike, laptop, pet, dog, cat, brother, sister, job, idea, problem, question
- **Practice:** drills → interview mini-dialogue
- **Production:** 5 предложений *I've got… / I haven't got…*
- **Phrases:** *Have you got a minute? I've got an idea.*

### Lesson 8 — Food, café & Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** review a/an + some (intro light); would like — preview phrase only
- **Vocab (14):** menu, order, tea, juice, bread, cheese, salad, soup, chicken, fish, bill, cup, glass, bottle
- **Practice:** match food→picture → cloze dialogue in café
- **Production:** dialogue «In a café» (заказ + thank you)
- **Unit Challenge:** описать кухню + заказ напитка/еды
- **Travel seed:** *Can I have a coffee, please? The bill, please.*

## Unit 3 — Daily life (Present Simple)

### Lesson 9 — Present Simple (I/you/we/they)
- **Grammar:** Present Simple affirmative & negative (don't)
- **Vocab (12):** live, work, study, like, love, want, need, speak, eat, drink, walk, cook
- **Practice:** form drills → true/false about yourself → cloze
- **Production:** 6 предложений о своей жизни
- **Input:** audio — «I live in…»

### Lesson 10 — Present Simple (he/she/it)
- **Grammar:** -s / -es / -ies; doesn't
- **Vocab (12):** get up, go, come, start, finish, watch, read, write, play, listen, help, call
- **Practice:** he/she transforms → error fix (*She live…*)
- **Production:** рассказать о дне друга/партнёра
- **Input:** text 70 слов — «Anna's day»

### Lesson 11 — Present Simple questions + Wh-
- **Grammar:** Do/Does…?; What / Where / When / Who / Why / How
- **Vocab (12):** how often, what time, where, when, why, because, usually, after, before, lunch, dinner, home
- **Practice:** make questions → answer bank → interview
- **Production:** 6 вопросов однокурснику / «другу в приложении»
- **Phrases:** *What do you do? Where do you live?*

### Lesson 12 — Routine & Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** review Present Simple + time expressions
- **Vocab (12):** wake up, have breakfast, have lunch, have dinner, go to work, go home, sleep, shower, commute, busy, free, break
- **Practice:** order the day → cloze → listen for times
- **Production:** «My typical day» (8 предложений / 1 мин речи)
- **Unit Challenge:** quiz + speaking day routine
- **Input:** listening — day of a Londoner (slow)

## Unit 4 — Ability, city & how often

### Lesson 13 — can / can't
- **Grammar:** can / can't; Can you…?; short answers
- **Vocab (12):** swim, drive, cook, dance, sing, speak English, help, open, wait, see, hear, understand
- **Practice:** ability drills → polite requests with can
- **Production:** 4 умения + 2 просьбы в диалоге
- **Travel seed:** *Can you help me? Can I pay by card?*

### Lesson 14 — Imperatives & directions
- **Grammar:** Imperative (+ don't); follow instructions
- **Vocab (14):** go, turn left, turn right, straight on, stop, wait, take, cross, street, corner, map, near, next to, opposite
- **Practice:** map tasks → reorder directions → listen & choose route
- **Production:** дать дорогу от станции до кафе (4–6 шагов)
- **Travel focus:** asking & giving directions

### Lesson 15 — Adverbs of frequency
- **Grammar:** always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never + word order
- **Vocab (12):** always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day, once, twice, week, month, habit
- **Practice:** place adverb → survey questions → true sentences
- **Production:** «How often…?» мини-интервью (5 Q&A)
- **Input:** audio habits dialogue

### Lesson 16 — City & transport + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** review can + imperatives + frequency
- **Vocab (14):** bus, train, metro, taxi, station, stop, ticket, fare, centre, park, museum, shop, hotel, airport
- **Practice:** collocations (*take a bus, get off*) → scenario dialogue
- **Production:** «How I get to work / centre»
- **Unit Challenge:** спросить дорогу + сказать, как часто ездишь на метро
- **Input:** listening — tourist asks for the station

## Unit 5 — Place, time & now

### Lesson 17 — Prepositions of place
- **Grammar:** in, on, at, under, next to, between, behind, in front of, near
- **Vocab (12):** left, right, middle, shelf, desk, pocket, bag, building, entrance, exit, bridge, square
- **Practice:** picture prepositions → hide & seek sentences
- **Production:** где лежат 5 вещей в комнате
- **Travel seed:** *The hotel is next to the station.*

### Lesson 18 — Prepositions of time
- **Grammar:** in (months/years/parts of day), on (days/dates), at (clock times / night / weekend BrE note)
- **Vocab (12):** Monday–Sunday, January (select), birthday, holiday, night, noon, midnight, date, appointment, meeting
- **Practice:** in/on/at choose → calendar cloze
- **Production:** рассказать о своей неделе с предлогами
- **Phrases:** *on Monday, at 7, in the morning*

### Lesson 19 — Present Continuous
- **Grammar:** am/is/are + V-ing; spelling; negatives & questions
- **Vocab (12):** doing, looking, wearing, sitting, standing, waiting, talking, working, raining, shining, happening, now
- **Practice:** form drills → picture description → What's happening?
- **Production:** описать 1 картинку (6 предложений)
- **Input:** audio «What are you doing?»

### Lesson 20 — Simple vs Continuous + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** Present Simple (habits) vs Present Continuous (now)
- **Vocab (12):** usually, now, right now, today, this week, always, at the moment, strange, normal, busy, free, tired
- **Practice:** choose tense → error fix → contrast pairs
- **Production:** «Usually I… but today I am…»
- **Unit Challenge:** quiz + picture story now vs routine
- **Input:** short text contrast day

## Unit 6 — Past, plans & first stories

### Lesson 21 — was / were
- **Grammar:** was / were; wasn't / weren't; Was/Were…?
- **Vocab (12):** yesterday, last night, last week, ago, at home, at work, ill, happy, sad, late, early, away
- **Practice:** am→was transforms → Yes/No questions
- **Production:** где ты был вчера в 3 точках времени
- **Input:** audio «Where were you yesterday?»

### Lesson 22 — Past Simple regular
- **Grammar:** V-ed; spelling; pronunciation /t/ /d/ /ɪd/ awareness
- **Vocab (12):** worked, lived, liked, wanted, needed, watched, played, cooked, visited, stayed, walked, called
- **Practice:** verb→past → cloze weekend story
- **Production:** 5 предложений о прошлом уикенде (regular)
- **Input:** micro-text 80 слов

### Lesson 23 — Past Simple irregular (core)
- **Grammar:** high-frequency irregulars (affirmative)
- **Vocab / verbs (14):** went, came, had, did, saw, got, took, made, ate, drank, bought, met, said, left
- **Practice:** match present↔past → gap story → listen & tick verbs
- **Production:** «Yesterday I…» с ≥6 irregular
- **Input:** listening story «A day in the city»

### Lesson 24 — Past questions, plans & Level Review
- **Grammar:** Did you…?; Wh- past; going to (plans); would like / want / need (functional)
- **Vocab (14):** tomorrow, next week, plan, trip, museum, ticket, rain, enjoy, fun, tired, hungry, thirsty, ready, later
- **Practice:** past questions → going to plans → café *I'd like…*
- **Production A:** «My last weekend» (6–8 предложений)  
- **Production B:** «My plans for next weekend»
- **Level Challenge:** mixed quiz Units 1–6 + speaking board (3 prompts) + micro-story reading 100–120 слов
- **Bridge to A2:** graded micro-story + travel phrase pack checklist

### Travel Survival pack (A1 exit)
- *Excuse me. Can you help me?*
- *Where is the station / toilet / hotel?*
- *How much is it?*
- *A table for two, please. The bill, please.*
- *I don't understand. Can you speak slowly?*
- *I'm looking for…*
- *Turn left / right. Go straight on.*
