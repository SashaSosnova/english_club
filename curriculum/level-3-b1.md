# Level 3 — Confidence (B1)

**Уроков:** 32 (8 units × 4)  
**Длина урока:** 30–40 мин  
**Цель уровня:** уверенный разговор на бытовые и часть абстрактных тем; graded/YA литература; фильмы с EN-субтитрами; неожиданные ситуации abroad.

**К концу уровня ученик умеет:**
- рассказать связную историю (Past Continuous / Perfect / narrative);
- обсудить фильм/книгу: plot, characters, opinion;
- справляться с нестандартными travel-ситуациями;
- читать adapted short stories / graded B1;
- смотреть сцены 2–5 мин с EN-subs и пересказывать.

---

## Unit 1 — Narrative tenses

### Lesson 1 — Present Perfect Continuous
- **Grammar:** have/has been + V-ing; since / for; contrast with Present Perfect Simple
- **Vocab (12):** for, since, lately, recently, all day, tired, working on, waiting, learning, improving, progress, still
- **Practice:** since/for drills → choose PPC vs PPS
- **Production:** «I've been learning English for… / I've learned…»
- **Input:** interview audio about hobbies

### Lesson 2 — Past Continuous
- **Grammar:** was/were + V-ing; interrupted actions; background
- **Vocab (12):** while, when, as, suddenly, background, happen, ring, knock, rain, shine, drive, walk
- **Practice:** picture stories → when/while gaps
- **Production:** «Yesterday at 8 I was… when…»
- **Literature seed:** setting a scene

### Lesson 3 — Past Perfect (storytelling)
- **Grammar:** had + V3 before another past action
- **Vocab (12):** already, before, after, by the time, because, realise, forget, leave, miss, lose, arrive, too late
- **Practice:** timeline ordering → combine sentences
- **Production:** anecdote with ≥2 Past Perfect
- **Input:** short story paragraph — identify tenses

### Lesson 4 — Narrative mix + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** Past Simple + Continuous + Perfect in one story
- **Vocab (12):** suddenly, meanwhile, eventually, in the end, luckily, unfortunately, to my surprise, at first, later, finally, plot, event
- **Practice:** rebuild jumbled story → tense clinic
- **Production:** personal story 12–15 предложений / 2 мин речи
- **Unit Challenge:** retell a graded story using all three past forms
- **Input:** adapted short story excerpt (gist + tense hunt)

## Unit 2 — Hypotheticals & voice

### Lesson 5 — Second Conditional
- **Grammar:** If + Past Simple, would + V; were for all persons (advice)
- **Vocab (12):** would, could, imagine, dream, advice, rich, abroad, fluent, chance, risk, decide, change
- **Practice:** dream life prompts → advice (*If I were you…*)
- **Production:** 8 hypotheticals about travel/life
- **Speaking:** «If you could live anywhere…»

### Lesson 6 — Third Conditional (intro)
- **Grammar:** If + Past Perfect, would have + V3
- **Vocab (12):** regret, mistake, missed opportunity, should have (preview), luckily, if only (preview), delayed, forgotten, chosen, accepted
- **Practice:** rewrite regrets → travel near-miss stories
- **Production:** 5 regrets / alternate pasts
- **Input:** podcast-style anecdote (learner speed)

### Lesson 7 — Passive Past & Perfect
- **Grammar:** was/were + V3; has/have been + V3 (common forms)
- **Vocab (12):** built, written, directed, based on, awarded, released, stolen, damaged, repaired, invented, discovered, known as
- **Practice:** film/book facts in passive → news light
- **Production:** 6 facts about a film/book/city
- **Film/Book vocab seed:** *was directed by, was based on*

### Lesson 8 — Modals of deduction + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** must / might / may / can't + be / have been (light)
- **Vocab (12):** must be, might be, can't be, probably, definitely, evidence, clue, strange, obvious, possible, impossible, sure
- **Practice:** photo deductions → mystery micro-text
- **Production:** speculate about a film still / book cover
- **Unit Challenge:** hypothetical advice + regret + deductions about a scene
- **Input:** silent film stills / scene without audio — speculate then watch

## Unit 3 — Reporting, patterns & description

### Lesson 9 — Reported speech (statements)
- **Grammar:** backshift; say vs tell; time/place changes (today→that day)
- **Vocab (12):** said, told, explained, admitted, claimed, promised, mentioned, according to, news, interview, quote, rumour
- **Practice:** direct→reported → news transform
- **Production:** пересказать новость / диалог (8 линий)
- **Input:** short interview audio

### Lesson 10 — Reported questions & requests
- **Grammar:** ask if/whether; ask + Wh; ask/tell someone to + V
- **Vocab (12):** asked, wondered, wanted to know, politely, urgently, remind, warn, advise, invite, refuse, accept, order
- **Practice:** airport/hotel reported requests → roleplay then report
- **Production:** report a difficult customer dialogue
- **Travel:** dealing with staff via reported summary

### Lesson 11 — Gerunds vs infinitives
- **Grammar:** patterns — decide to, want to, enjoy -ing, stop to/-ing (intro contrast), look forward to -ing
- **Vocab (14):** decide, hope, plan, promise, manage, fail, enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, keep, suggest, recommend, look forward to
- **Practice:** pattern sorting → error clinic
- **Production:** plans & hobbies with correct patterns
- **Input:** advice article (simplified)

### Lesson 12 — Relative clauses (defining) + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** who/which/that/where; omit object pronoun (light)
- **Vocab (12):** whose (intro), where, that, character, setting, author, director, scene, chapter, theme, relationship, detail
- **Practice:** expand notes into descriptions → book blurbs
- **Production:** describe a person and a place with relative clauses
- **Unit Challenge:** report a conversation + describe a character from reader
- **Input:** graded B1 chapter — character map

## Unit 4 — Precision: articles, quantity, wishes

### Lesson 13 — Articles advanced
- **Grammar:** zero article; institutions; unique objects; general vs specific
- **Vocab (12):** society, nature, life, prison, hospital, university, the internet, the weather, the same, the only, a few, a little
- **Practice:** article surgery on learner texts
- **Production:** write & self-correct a 100-word paragraph
- **Input:** opinion paragraph with article focus

### Lesson 14 — Quantifiers & determiners
- **Grammar:** much/many/a lot of/lots of/plenty of; a few/few; a little/little; each/every/all/both/neither/none
- **Vocab (12):** plenty, several, various, hardly any, almost all, none, either, neither, both, each, every, whole
- **Practice:** choose quantifier → travel packing / city life tasks
- **Production:** compare two cities with quantifiers
- **Speaking:** «In my city there are plenty of… but few…»

### Lesson 15 — wish / if only (light)
- **Grammar:** wish + Past Simple (present); wish + Past Perfect (past); wish + would (annoyance, light)
- **Vocab (12):** wish, if only, regret, annoy, complain, louder, quieter, earlier, better, different, change, instead
- **Practice:** rewrite complaints → empathy dialogues
- **Production:** 6 wishes about language/travel/life
- **Literature seed:** character desires

### Lesson 16 — Linking for stories & arguments + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar / discourse:** although, even though, however, therefore, as a result, on the other hand, in addition
- **Vocab (12):** although, however, therefore, whereas, meanwhile, as a result, in addition, for instance, in conclusion, argument, point, reason
- **Practice:** upgrade a weak opinion text → debate prompts
- **Production:** short opinion (120–150 слов): books vs films
- **Unit Challenge:** article+quantifier clinic + opinion essay light
- **Input:** two short opposing reviews

## Unit 6 — People, money, culture

### Lesson 21 — Relationships & personality
- **Grammar:** modifiers (quite, rather, really); used to vs be used to (contrast light)
- **Vocab (14):** funny, reliable, patient, ambitious, generous, jealous, close, get on with, fall out, argue, support, trust, stranger, partner
- **Practice:** personality quizzes → describe characters
- **Production:** describe two people you know / two characters
- **Literature:** character comparison

### Lesson 22 — Money & services
- **Grammar:** passives in services (*I had my phone fixed* — causative intro light); need to / should have
- **Vocab (14):** cash, account, transfer, fee, refund, insurance, claim, budget, afford, waste, save, borrow, lend, charge
- **Practice:** bank/tourist office scenarios
- **Production:** explain a money problem and solution

### Lesson 23 — Culture, hobbies & small talk

- **Vocab (12):** festival, tradition, exhibition, gig, series, podcast, hobby, into, keen on, by the way, anyway, that reminds me
- **Practice:** small-talk chains → follow-up questions
- **Production:** 3-minute small talk simulation
- **Input:** party / café conversation audio

### Lesson 24 — News light + Unit Challenge
- **Grammar:** present perfect for recent news; passive in headlines
- **Vocab (12):** headline, report, according to, break out, announce, cancel, protest, celebrate, update, source, reliable, fake
- **Practice:** rewrite headlines → summarise a short news text
- **Production:** 80–100 word news summary + opinion
- **Unit Challenge:** people + money + news speaking stations

## Unit 7 — Books, films & deeper listening

### Lesson 25 — Film & book vocabulary
- **Lexis:** plot, character, scene, chapter, ending, twist, genre, comedy, drama, thriller, based on, spoil, subtitle, soundtrack
- **Grammar:** review narrative tenses in reviews
- **Practice:** label review parts → spoiler-free summary
- **Production:** review of a film/episode/book (150 слов)
- **Input:** trailer or scene 2–3 мин (EN subs)

### Lesson 26 — Scene lab (watching skills)
- **Skills:** gist → detail → language mining → shadowing → retell
- **Vocab:** from the scene (8–12 items mined live)
- **Practice:** ordered watching tasks; pause & predict
- **Production:** retell scene without looking at subs
- **Input:** 3–5 мин scene; second pass without subs

### Lesson 27 — Short story workshop
- **Skills:** read → vocab mine → comprehension → discuss themes
- **Grammar:** relative clauses + linking in discussion
- **Vocab:** theme, moral, narrator, conflict, ending, symbol (light), message, atmosphere
- **Practice:** 5 open questions + quote explain
- **Production:** alternate ending (120–150 слов)
- **Input:** adapted short story (B1)

### Lesson 28 — Idioms light + Unit Challenge
- **Lexis (10–12):** piece of cake, under the weather, hit the road, once in a blue moon, break the ice, cost an arm and a leg, on the same page, a big deal, catch someone's eye, at the end of the day
- **Practice:** meaning in context only (no isolated lists)
- **Production:** short dialogue using 4 idioms naturally
- **Unit Challenge:** film review + scene retell + idiom dialogue
- **Rule:** idioms only after context scenes; max 10–12 per unit cluster

## Unit 8 — Problems abroad & B1 exit

- **Practice:** branched scenario: lost bag / overbooked hotel
- **Production:** polite but firm complaint monologue
- **Travel capstone practice**

### Lesson 30 — Persuasion & negotiation

- **Vocab (12):** discount, upgrade, include, instead, otherwise, fair, deal, offer, suggest, compromise, prefer, rather
- **Practice:** roleplay tourist vs staff

### Lesson 31 — Extended speaking & writing

- **Prompts:** memorable journey / book that changed me / living abroad
- **Practice:** plan → speak → write → language upgrade
- **Focus:** narrative tenses + linkers + collocations

### Lesson 32 — Level Review & bridge to B2

- **Level Challenge / exit exam**
