# Level 4 — Fluency Bridge (B2 light)

**Уроков / модулей:** 24 (6 units × 4)  
**Длина урока:** 30–40 мин (+ отдельный input day 20–40 мин recommended)  
**Цель уровня:** художественная литература с словарём; фильмы/сериалы без RU-субтитров; уверенное общение в поездках и на абстрактные бытовые темы.

**К концу уровня ученик умеет:**
- читать YA / contemporary light fiction и simplified classics с активным словарём;
- смотреть эпизод/кусок фильма без RU (EN optional) и обсуждать;
- держать 5–8 мин монолога и живой диалог с уточнениями;
- замечать register (formal/informal) и смягчать речь;
- самостоятельно вести extensive reading + watching habit.

> Формат чуть свободнее, чем A1–B1: больше **модулей навыков** (Book Club / Scene Lab / Debate), грамматика — в сервисе fluency, не как единственный центр урока.

## Unit 1 — Style, register & nuance

### Lesson 1 — Formal vs informal English
- **Focus:** register in emails, service talks, friends
- **Lexis:** expand, request, enquire, get back to, ASAP, kinda, wanna (awareness), polite frames
- **Grammar in service:** question softening (*I was wondering if…*)
- **Practice:** rewrite informal→formal and back
- **Production:** email complaint + WhatsApp version of same problem
- **Travel:** polite escalation with hotel/airline

### Lesson 2 — Hedges & softeners
- **Focus:** maybe, sort of, a bit, quite, rather, I'm not sure but…, It seems…
- **Lexis:** softeners + disagreement without conflict
- **Practice:** blunt→soft transforms → debate micro-turns
- **Production:** disagree politely about a film ending
- **Input:** podcast disagreement excerpt

### Lesson 3 — Vague language & fillers (controlled)
- **Focus:** thing, stuff, kind of, and so on, or something; strategic fillers without mumbling
- **Practice:** describe unknown words (circumlocution)
- **Production:** 2-min talk describing objects/plots without key nouns
- **Skill:** survive when vocabulary fails abroad

### Lesson 4 — Tone in stories + Unit Challenge
- **Focus:** narrative voice — understatement, emphasis, humour light
- **Lexis:** actually, basically, literally (awareness), in fact, as it turned out
- **Practice:** rewrite flat story with tone markers
- **Production:** tell an embarrassing travel story with softener+tone
- **Unit Challenge:** register board — 3 audiences, same message

## Unit 2 — Advanced multi-word English

### Lesson 5 — Phrasal verbs set 3 (abstract)
- **Lexis:** bring about, carry on, come across, cut down on, figure out, get over, make up for, put forward, turn out, work out, look into, run into
- **Practice:** news/story gaps → paraphrase with single verbs and back
- **Production:** explain a problem using ≥6 phrasals
- **SRS:** Phrasals Abstract

### Lesson 6 — Idioms for everyday talk (contextual)
- **Lexis (10):** on the fence, read between the lines, get the hang of, call it a day, spill the beans, hit the nail on the head, out of the blue, keep an eye on, in hot water, the tip of the iceberg
- **Rule:** only via dialogues/scenes; personalised examples
- **Production:** short story using 4 idioms
- **Input:** sitcom/scene 2 мин — spot idioms

### Lesson 7 — Collocations for arguments & reviews
- **Lexis:** strong evidence, raise an issue, draw a conclusion, mixed feelings, highly recommend, deeply moved, utterly boring, widely known, fiercely debate, take into account
- **Practice:** upgrade weak reviews
- **Production:** 180-word review with advanced collocations

### Lesson 8 — Paraphrase lab + Unit Challenge
- **Skill:** say it another way (exam + real life)
- **Practice:** paraphrase prompts; avoid translation traps
- **Production:** explain a book plot twice — simple then rich
- **Unit Challenge:** phrasal/idiom/collocation mixed quiz + paraphrase battle

## Unit 3 — Book Club lite

### Lesson 9 — How to read extensively
- **Skills:** ignore non-essential unknown words; vocab notebook rules (max 10–12/chapter)
- **Practice:** timed reading + gist only
- **Production:** 5-sentence chapter summary
- **Setup:** choose Book A (YA/contemporary light)

### Lesson 10 — Character deep dive
- **Focus:** personality, motivation, relationships, change
- **Lexis:** motivate, conflict, reliable narrator, flaw, bond, betray, grow, struggle
- **Grammar:** relative clauses + modals of deduction about characters
- **Production:** character profile 150–180 слов
- **Discussion prompts:** Would you trust X? Why?

### Lesson 11 — Theme & atmosphere
- **Focus:** themes (love, fear, freedom, belonging…); atmosphere vocabulary
- **Lexis:** tense, gloomy, light-hearted, hopeful, unsettling, bittersweet, pace, tone
- **Practice:** quote→theme match
- **Production:** «The book feels… because…» monologue 3 мин
- **Input:** author interview / blurb comparison

### Lesson 12 — Book Club session + Unit Challenge
- **Format:** discussion circle simulation in app
- **Tasks:** opinion, disagreement, quote reaction, predict next chapter
- **Writing:** short essay 200 слов — Should X have done Y?
- **Unit Challenge:** chapter test (no dictionary) + discussion score rubric
- **Bridge:** start Book B or continue Book A

## Unit 4 — Scene Lab & listening stamina

### Lesson 13 — Watching without RU subs
- **Skills:** gist first; visual cues; tolerate ambiguity
- **Practice:** scene 5–7 мин — questions before details
- **Production:** 2-min scene summary
- **Rule:** RU subs off; EN optional first pass

### Lesson 14 — Dialogue mining & shadowing
- **Skills:** extract 8 chunks; shadow rhythm/intonation
- **Practice:** gap transcript → shadow lines → reuse in new dialogue
- **Production:** act alternative version of the scene
- **Focus:** natural reductions awareness (gonna/wanna — recognize)

### Lesson 15 — Episode stamina
- **Skills:** 10–15 мин authentic stretch with checkpoints
- **Practice:** pause every 5 мин — gist card
- **Production:** episode log: characters, conflict, 10 phrases
- **Habit builder:** weekly episode slot

### Lesson 16 — Film Club session + Unit Challenge
- **Discussion:** plot, motives, favourite line, culture notes
- **Writing:** review 200–220 слов without spoilers option
- **Unit Challenge:** watch new 7-мин clip cold → quiz + retell
- **Input:** comedy vs drama contrast scenes

## Unit 5 — Ideas, culture & debate

### Lesson 17 — Structuring long turns
- **Focus:** signposting — first of all, another point, that said, to sum up
- **Practice:** 4-min talks with outline cards
- **Production:** «How travel changes people»
- **Grammar:** cleft light (*What I liked was…*)

### Lesson 18 — Debate soft skills
- **Focus:** concede + counter (*That's true, but…*); ask for clarification
- **Topics:** subtitles vs dubbing; books vs adaptations; living abroad
- **Production:** paired debate 6–8 мин (or bot turns)
- **Lexis:** argue, claim, evidence, overrate, underrate, nuance

### Lesson 19 — Culture & pragmatics
- **Focus:** indirectness, politeness norms, small talk taboos awareness
- **Scenarios:** dinner invitation, refusing, complaining, humour
- **Production:** choose appropriate response in 8 situations
- **Travel:** cultural repair strategies (*Sorry, what I meant was…*)

### Lesson 20 — Current topics light + Unit Challenge
- **Focus:** explain a simple news/culture topic in English
- **Skills:** summarise → react → personal link
- **Production:** 180-word reaction post
- **Unit Challenge:** long turn + debate + culture situation test

## Unit 6 — Capstone: independent learner

### Lesson 21 — Error clinic (personal)
- **Focus:** top personal grammar/vocab mistakes from app stats
- **Practice:** targeted drills + reformulation
- **Production:** rewrite an old A2/B1 text at B2 level
- **Meta:** learning plan for next 30 days

### Lesson 22 — Travel fluency workshop
- **Scenarios stacked:** delay + complaint + negotiation + small talk + directions + medical
- **Twist cards:** rude customer, no Wi-Fi, misunderstood accent
- **Production:** full travel day role-play sequence
- **Success:** solve problems without switching to RU

### Lesson 23 — Literature & film capstone
- **Tasks:**
  1. Read a chapter cold → summary + 10 useful chunks  
  2. Watch 10–12 мин without RU → discussion  
  3. Compare book vs screen (if same story) or theme across both
- **Writing:** 220–250 слов comparative reflection

### Lesson 24 — Level Review & beyond
- **Assessment:**
  - Listening stamina clip quiz  
  - Reading chapter quiz  
  - 5-min monologue (recorded rubric)  
  - Travel scenario battery  
  - Writing 220 слов
- **Beyond B2 light:** extensive reading list, series plan, conversation goals, optional exam path (FCE-ish skills without exam pressure)

## Level 4 — параллельные привычки

### Weekly rhythm (recommended)
| Day | Focus |
|-----|--------|
| 3× | Course lessons (30–40 мин) |
| 1× | Book Club reading (20–40 мин) |
| 1× | Episode / film chunk (20–40 мин) |
| Daily | SRS 8–10 мин (phrasals, chunks, mined words) |

### Reading ladder
1. Finish one graded B2 / easy YA novel  
2. One contemporary light novel or short story collection  
3. One simplified classic → attempt original opening chapters  

### Watching ladder
1. Sitcom episodes (clear dialogue)  
2. Drama scenes with clearer diction  
3. Films with mixed accents — EN subs only if needed  
4. Same film chunk second time: no subs  

### Chunk notebook (instead of word lists)
For each input day capture:
- 8–12 chunks (not isolated words)  
- 1 pronunciation note  
- 1 reusable dialogue line  

### B2 exit criteria
- Read a YA chapter with ≤10 lookups and 70%+ comprehension  
- Watch 10 мин without RU and retell accurately  
- 5-min monologue with signposting and softener control  
- Handle stacked travel problems in English  
- Maintain 4-week streak of reading+watching habit
